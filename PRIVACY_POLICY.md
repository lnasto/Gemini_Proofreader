Gemini Proofreader Pro fully respects the privacy of its users. Please read the policy below to understand how your data is handled.



1\. Data Collection



The creator of this extension DOES NOT collect, DOES NOT store, and DOES NOT have access to any of your personal data, the texts you select, or your browsing history.



2\. Where does your data go?



The extension acts as a "bridge" between your browser and Google. When you select text and request an action (e.g., Proofread), that text is sent encrypted (via HTTPS) directly to the official Google Gemini API servers (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). For how Google manages data on its API, please refer to the Google API Terms of Service.



3\. API Key Management (Security)



The Google AI API Key you enter in the extension settings is stored exclusively locally on your device using chrome.storage.local technology. Your key is not shared, synced to the cloud by us, or sent anywhere other than directly communicating with Google to authenticate your requests.
